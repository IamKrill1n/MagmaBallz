## A. Foundations and meta-results

### A1. Birkhoff completeness
Semantic consequence equals finite syntactic derivability (refl/symm/trans/substitution/congruence):
`Γ ⊢ w≃w' ⟺ Γ ⊨ w≃w'`.
- **Side:** meta; licenses every TRUE technique (proof enumeration is a complete semi-decision procedure).
- **Applicability:** universal.
- **Source:** Birkhoff, "On the structure of abstract algebras", Proc. Cambridge Philos. Soc. 31 (1935); formalized as [BP] Basic theory ch. 1, Thm 1.8, https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/blueprint/basic-theory-chapter.html; [Repo] `equational_theories/Completeness.lean`.
- **Status here:** implicitly everywhere; `generalized` implements the five inference rules as an explicit replayable derivation kernel (`refl/hypothesis/symmetry/transitivity/congruence` + `replay_derivation`).
- **Executable:** Semi.

### A2. Free magma relative to a theory (canonical-model criterion)
`E1 ⊨ u≃v` iff u and v have equal images in the free magma of Mod(E1); otherwise that free magma is itself a countermodel.
- **Side:** both (in principle a full decision — modulo the word problem being undecidable in general).
- **Applicability:** universal; computational only when the quotient is tractable (see F3, F4).
- **Source:** [BP] Abstract nonsense ch. 10, Thms 10.2–10.3, Cor 10.4, https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/blueprint/abstract-nonsense-chapter.html.
- **Status here:** no local solver.
- **Executable:** No in general; Yes when a confluent presentation exists (F3).

### A3. HSP / variety closure
Mod(E1) is closed under homomorphic images, subalgebras, products — so products of premise-models are premise-models, and a countermodel can be shrunk to the submagma generated by the falsifying assignment.
- **Side:** FALSE-side amplifier + minimization.
- **Applicability:** universal.
- **Source:** Birkhoff 1935 (variety theorem); usage surveyed in local `research/equational-implication-strategies-and-theorems.md` §"HSP closure".
- **Status here:** product tables in emily-family (`_product_tables`) and suii0x (`product_tables`); no local solver does submagma shrinking of found tables.
- **Executable:** Yes-bounded.

### A4. Compactness
An implication from an infinite theory follows from a finite subset.
- **Side:** meta. **Applicability:** theory-level questions (single-premise problems unaffected).
- **Source:** [BP] ch. 1 Cor 1.9; [Repo] `equational_theories/Compactness.lean`.
- **Status here:** none (not needed for single-hypothesis problems).
- **Executable:** n/a.

### A5. Undecidability of the general problem
For one binary operation: whether a single identity has a nontrivial model, and whether it has a *finite* nontrivial model, are undecidable (recursively inseparable) — via `E1 ⊨ (x=y)` this makes unrestricted `E1 ⊨ E2` and `E1 ⊨fin E2` undecidable. Perkins established undecidability for finitely presented equational theories generally.
- **Side:** meta (no complete solver can exist; portfolio + budgets is forced).
- **Source:** McKenzie, "On spectra, and the negative solution of the decision problem for identities having a finite nontrivial model", J. Symbolic Logic 40 (1975) 186–195, https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-symbolic-logic/article/abs/on-spectra-and-the-negative-solution-of-the-decision-problem-for-identities-having-a-finite-nontrivial-model1/101AF2F2F2858A6265074FD677B154E0; Perkins, "Unsolvable problems for equational theories", Notre Dame J. Formal Logic 8 (1967) 175–185. (Perkins's exact corollary-by-corollary scoping for the single-identity ⊨ single-identity sharpening: UNVERIFIED in detail; McNulty, "A Field Guide to Equational Logic", J. Symb. Comput. 14 (1992) is the map.)
- **Status here:** design assumption in all solvers.
- **Executable:** n/a.

### A6. Austin laws and Austin pairs (finite ≠ general)
An *Austin law* has infinite models but no nontrivial finite model; an *Austin pair* (E1,E2) satisfies `E1 ⊨fin E2` but not `E1 ⊨ E2`. ETP: 820 (or 822, pending E677) such pairs among order ≤ 4 laws; ten proven order-5 Austin laws (E4916, 15535, 17522, 20034, 22455, 22818, 25964, 28770, 30591, 41082); E28770 `x = (((y◇y)◇y)◇x)◇(y◇z)` is the shortest Austin law; E5093 conjectural. Flagship pair: Asterix E65 vs Obelix E1491 (each finitely implies the other; neither does generally). Consequence for solvers: exhausting finite model sizes never proves TRUE, and for an Austin pair no finite FALSE certificate exists at all.
- **Side:** meta / FALSE-side boundary.
- **Source:** A. K. Austin, "A note on models of identities", Proc. AMS 16 (1965) 522–523 (original identity formula UNVERIFIED — text not retrieved); Kisielewicz, "Austin identities", Algebra Universalis 38 (1997); [Paper] §1.3, §8, Problem 8.1; [BP] Infinite-model ch. 5 (Thms 5.1–5.4, Props 5.8–5.14) and Order-5 Austin laws ch. 20, https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/blueprint/order-5-austin-laws.html; ETP wiki "Tour of selected equations".
- **Status here:** no local solver detects Austin-pair risk; reja's infinite-model artifact channel (G6) is the only mechanism that could even express such a FALSE.
- **Executable:** detecting "no finite model ≤ n" is Yes-bounded; proving Austin-ness is Heuristic.

### A7. Spectrum of a law
The set of finite model sizes of a law is multiplicatively closed and contains 1; famous constraints: central groupoids exist only at orders n² (E4); Austin laws have spectrum {1}. ETP determined which of the 4694 laws have full spectrum: 3074 do; 1558 lack size-2 models, 62 lack size-3; E1286/E2301 have smallest nontrivial model size 7.
- **Side:** FALSE-side search pruning (skip sizes not in the hypothesis's spectrum) + triage.
- **Applicability:** any pair where E1's spectrum is known/derivable.
- **Source:** [Paper] §9 (full-spectrum campaign, ±1/0 linear scan covering 3068 laws, 7 ad hoc residual constructions), §5.1 Table 1; Knuth 1970 (E4 below); Stein, "Finite models of identities", Proc. AMS 14 (1963) 216–222 (contents UNVERIFIED); [Repo] `Generated/FullSpectrum.lean`, `scripts/full_spectrum_bruteforce.rb`.
- **Status here:** the only spectrum-aware code is m00006's CG9 comment/logic (witness table search extended to 9 *because* central groupoids only exist at n²). No general spectrum reasoning in any local solver.
- **Executable:** per-size check Yes-bounded; proving a spectrum constraint is Heuristic.

## B. Triage and infrastructure

### B1. Canonical numbering / normal form of laws
Quotient laws by variable relabeling and symmetry of ≃, well-order by size then shape: 4694 laws of order ≤ 4; 57,882 of order 5; 298,012,537 of order ≤ 8.
- **Side:** triage/infra. **Applicability:** universal.
- **Source:** [Paper] App. A (counts; OEIS A376640); [BP] ch. 1 Lemmas 1.12–1.14; [Repo] `scripts/count_magma_eqns/`.
- **Status here:** all solvers alpha-normalize variables; eulerv5/dufius key oracle data by equation ID.
- **Executable:** Yes.

### B2. Duality
`E1 ⊨ E2 ⟺ dual(E1) ⊨ dual(E2)` where dual reverses the operation (opposite magma). Halves all work; transposing a table dualizes a countermodel. 84 of 4694 laws are self-dual, 2305 dual pairs.
- **Side:** both (transfer). **Applicability:** universal.
- **Source:** [BP] General implications ch. 3, Lemma 3.5 (`Law.MagmaLaw.implies_iff_dual`), https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/blueprint/general-implications-chapter.html; [Repo] `equational_theories/MagmaOp.lean`, `Duals/`, `scripts/find_dual.py`.
- **Status here:** m00006 (`dual_term`, FALSE-side dual-problem retry with transposed witness); reja (`dual_equation`/`transpose_table`, `allow_dual` in structured search).
- **Executable:** Yes.

### B3. Transitive closure / reduction of the implication graph
⊨ is a preorder; positive implications compose, and a non-implication plus positive edges yields more non-implications (contrapositive). ETP formalized only 10,657 positive + 586,925 negative generators and recovered all 22,028,942 pairs by closure.
- **Side:** both (amplifier). **Applicability:** corpora with shared laws.
- **Source:** [BP] ch. 3 Lemma 3.2; [Paper] §1.3, §3; [Repo] `Closure.lean`, `scripts/transitive_closure.rb`, `transitive_reduction.rb`.
- **Status here:** emily-family (`_load_implication_graph` + `_find_transitive_path` + `try_transitive_library_proof` — replays known order-≤4 edges); eulerv5 (`_matrix`/oracle bitmatrix = the closed graph shipped as data); dufius S00007 (base85 oracle blobs of witnesses + simple rewrites). m00006 deliberately has no oracle graph.
- **Executable:** Yes.

### B4. One-witness-many-refutations ("Facts" pattern / satisfaction signatures)
Compute once the bitset of laws a magma satisfies; one magma then refutes every pair (satisfied, unsatisfied). ETP encoding: `@[equational_result] theorem X.Facts : ∃ (G : Type) (_ : Magma G), Facts G [sat…] [refuted…] := ⟨…, by decideFin!⟩`, where `Facts` elaborates to a conjunction of Equation and ¬Equation statements; harvested by `lake exe extract_implications`. This is how 524 magmas refute 96.3% of all false order-≤4 implications.
- **Side:** FALSE (infrastructure). **Applicability:** any explicit finite witness.
- **Source:** [Paper] §3 (Fig. 6), §5.1; [Repo] `equational_theories/FactsSyntax.lean` (with `calculate_facts`), example `CentralGroupoids.lean` lines 121–125 (`MagmaA2T.Facts`).
- **Status here:** every local solver keeps a witness bank probed pair-by-pair (m00006 named tables; eulerv5 `tables_satisfying_h` keyed by hypothesis ID), but none computes/persists full satisfaction signatures across a corpus (Marathon reuse is per-run).
- **Executable:** Yes-bounded.

### B5. ATP-vetted axiom strengthening ("search of compatible properties")
Guess an extra property P (unit element, idempotence, squares-closure, injectivity of a translation…); run an ATP on `E1 ∧ P ⊢ E2` for a long time; failure is heuristic license to search for countermodels *within* P (a much smaller space); success discards P. Also stepwise derived-axiom stacking to speed up Mace4 exhaustion by orders of magnitude (E1518 ⊭ E47: smallest model 15, first found at size 232).
- **Side:** triage-for-FALSE / search accelerant.
- **Applicability:** hard pairs where naive model search stalls.
- **Source:** [Paper] §7.3.7 (E1323 ⊭ E2744), §7.3.8 (Ex. 7.1), §5.5.
- **Status here:** none.
- **Executable:** Heuristic (mechanical subroutines).

### B6. ML implication predictors
Character-level CNN on equation pairs predicts implication truth at 99.7% (99.6% with 5% of training data); proposed to triage ATP effort on the order-5 graph. Never evidence, only ordering.
- **Side:** triage. **Applicability:** corpora with labeled history.
- **Source:** [Paper] §11 (Tables 5–6); [Repo] `scripts/predictor/`; transformer/GNN mentions [Paper] §11 fn. 27 (details UNVERIFIED).
- **Status here:** none (marathon `difficulty_score` in triage/fewshot demos is hand-crafted feature scoring, not learned).
- **Executable:** Yes to run; Heuristic as evidence.

### B7. Syntactic pre-filter cascades at scale
Cheap necessary-condition filters run in sequence to cut astronomically large candidate sets before expensive tools: shape filters, evaluation in a fixed reference model (e.g. (ℤ,−)), variable-parity filters, then accumulated finite countermodels, then ATP. ETP's Higman–Neumann hunt cut 298M laws to 213 candidates this way.
- **Side:** triage. **Applicability:** bulk sweeps (law hunts, corpus labeling).
- **Source:** [Paper] §10.1–10.3.
- **Status here:** in miniature: every solver's priority/routing tiers (m00006 `problem_priority`; dufius `classify`; reja invariant report as router).
- **Executable:** Yes per filter.

### B8. Most-wanted ranking / theorem power scoring
Rank candidate results by how many unknown pairs they would settle under closure (sparse-matrix closure of the implication graph).
- **Side:** triage. **Source:** [Repo] `scripts/find_powerful_theorems.py`, `scripts/generate_most_wanted_list.py`.
- **Status here:** none.
- **Executable:** Yes.

## C. Proving TRUE (general magmas)

### C1. Direct substitution / simple rewrites
E2 follows from a single substitution instance of E1 (proof term literally `λ x y … => h …`), or one instance under symmetry. ETP: 53,905 implications by this pattern alone.
- **Side:** TRUE. **Applicability:** E2's sides match E1's sides under one substitution.
- **Source:** [BP] Simple rewrites ch. 21, https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/blueprint/simple-rewrite-chapter.html; [Repo] `Generated/SimpleRewrites/` + `src/find_simple_rewrites.py`; [Paper] §6.1.
- **Status here:** m00006 (`direct_substitution_route`, incl. `.symm` case); dufius (`_try_auto_proof` — note SOLVER_COMPENDIUM §4 documents its swapped-trans-link bug); suii0x (`try_direct_substitution`, compound-term pool variant); generalized (unification-based `_direct_between`); emily-family (`try_direct_proof`).
- **Executable:** Yes-bounded.

### C2. Singleton / constant collapse and diagonalization
(a) A law `x = R` with x ∉ vars(R), or with a one-sided variable, forces a singleton magma (equivalent to E2 `x = y`), making everything true — 815 laws. (b) Diagonalization (disjoint variable sets on the two sides) shows the law implies constancy of each side over its arguments — 85 laws ≡ constant law E46. (c) Thm 3.7: E46 implies every law whose two sides both have order ≥ 1.
- **Side:** TRUE. **Applicability:** syntactically detectable shapes of E1.
- **Source:** [BP] ch. 3 Thms 3.6–3.7; ch. 8 (constant/singleton), https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/blueprint/constant-chapter.html; [Repo] `Generated/Constant.lean`, `Generated/Singleton.lean`; [Paper] §6.1.
- **Status here:** m00006 (`singleton_route` + four collapse routes: `middle_self_collapse`, `front_double_self_collapse`, `alternating_front_self_collapse`, mirrored variant; `c9_e1072_collapse_route`); dufius (`try_x1`, `emit_singleton_certificate`); emily-family (`try_singleton`, constancy engine `find_constancy_steps`/`build_constancy_proof`); eulerv5 (`_singleton_collapse`, `constancy_lemmas_v5`); reja (`rigidity_collapse_ladder_attempt`, `collapse_certificate_body` — derives the collapse *by critical pairs* and emits certified chains).
- **Executable:** Yes-bounded.

### C3. Bounded rewrite-chain search (BFS / meet-in-the-middle)
Apply E1 as a bidirectional rewrite at any subterm, search from one or both goal sides for a collision; render as calc/trans chains with congruence wrappers. ETP versions: brute-forced tiny tactic scripts (`apply h`, `rw [h]`, `rw [←h]`, `nth_rewrite` combos — ~4.5M transitive implications) and meet-in-the-middle "equation search" over the implication graph (~650K).
- **Side:** TRUE. **Applicability:** short proofs whose intermediate terms stay small; weak when E1 has fresh variables on one side (needs instantiation policy).
- **Source:** [BP] Trivial auto ch. 22, Equation search ch. 24, https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/blueprint/equation-search-chapter.html; [Repo] `Generated/TrivialBruteforce/` (`src/bruteforce*.rb`), `Generated/EquationSearch/` (`src/search.rb`); [Paper] §6.1 (~15,000 direct + ~4M closed).
- **Status here:** m00006 (`find_rewrite_chain` depth 2; `absorption_closure_route` depth 3; `equational_closure_route` bidirectional depth 4 with pool-filled free variables; `completed_bridge_route`; worktree adds `absorption_context_bridge_route`, `deep_absorption_closure_route`); eulerv5 (`proof_bfs_v5` — see SOLVER_COMPENDIUM §§5–6 for its near-miss scoring caveats); emily-family (`compute_bfs_near_miss`, `try_calc_chain_proof`, `try_rw_chain_proof`); suii0x (`try_calc_chain`, `try_one_congr_calc_chain`); generalized (`_short_chain_events`).
- **Executable:** Yes-bounded (complete only in the limit).

### C4. Knuth–Bendix completion → complete rewriting system → decision procedure
Orient E1 by a reduction order, add oriented critical-pair consequences until confluent; if completion terminates, normal-form comparison *decides* every `E1 ⊨ E2` (both verdicts). Historically born from the central-groupoid law. ETP: E477's confluent system (Thm 10.8); ChatGPT-guessed complete system for E3523 then formally verified, settling all implications from it.
- **Side:** both (full decision when it succeeds). **Applicability:** orientable E1 (fails on fresh-variable RHS, unorientable laws); termination not guaranteed.
- **Source:** Knuth & Bendix, "Simple Word Problems in Universal Algebras" (1970), https://www.cs.tufts.edu/~nr/cs257/archive/don-knuth/knuth-bendix.pdf; [BP] Rewriting ch. 19 (Newman's lemma, critical-pair criterion Thm 19.6); [Paper] §6.3, §11.
- **Status here:** reja implements *ordered completion* for TRUE discovery (`ordered_completion_discover` + dependency-closure replay `ordered_completion_replay` rendering Lean chains) — discovery only, no confluence certificate and no normal-form FALSE decisions. m00006's critical-pair lemma engine (`_critical_pair_candidates`, `derive_gap_lemmas`, `cp_saturation_route`) is bounded KB-style superposition for lemma generation, not completion. No local solver runs completion to convergence or exploits a completed system as a decision procedure.
- **Executable:** Semi (mechanical once found; finding the terminating system is Heuristic).

### C5. Superposition / unfailing-completion saturation ATPs
Negate E2 with Skolem constants, saturate with the superposition calculus (Vampire, E, Prover9, Duper). ETP's positive workhorse: Prover9 proves all positive order-≤4 implications with max_weight 55; Vampire 5.0 proves all true ETP implications; the project's Vampire pipeline contributed 1,775 formalized proofs during the campaign. Expert configuration lore (weights, SOS limits, strategy schedules, LRS) is part of the prior art.
- **Side:** TRUE (semi-decision; also FALSE via built-in finite model finders, see E5).
- **Applicability:** universal for TRUE.
- **Source:** Bachmair & Ganzinger, "Rewrite-based equational theorem proving with selection and simplification", J. Logic and Computation 4 (1994); Bachmair–Dershowitz–Plaisted, "Completion without failure" (1989); [Paper] §7.1.1, §7.3.2–7.3.5; [BP] Vampire ch. 26; [Repo] `Generated/VampireProven/` (`src/vampire_proofs.py`), `equational_theories/Superposition.lean` (Lean replay of superposition steps).
- **Status here:** reja's `pc_saturate` is a native bounded paramodulation/superposition saturation over the hypothesis (+aux lemmas), with `pc_render` emitting the full Lean derivation; m00006's `cp_saturation_route` is a goal-directed bounded relative (measured +6/−0 on its order-5 TRUE eval set). No local solver embeds a real ATP (sandbox forbids it); SOLVER_COMPENDIUM Phase 4 plans offline E-prover *distillation* into rules.
- **Executable:** Semi.

### C6. Equality saturation / e-graphs
Grow an e-graph of terms under E1-rewrites in both orientations without committing to a normal form, until the goal sides merge; extract an explanation and replay it. ETP: the Lean `egg` tactic (lean-egg) found 86 extra implications and re-derived the 1689 ≡ 2 chain; MagmaEgg (standalone Rust prover on the egg library) proved 5,574 of 24,283 attempted; fresh-variable RHS handled by instantiating from proof-context/goal subterms.
- **Side:** TRUE. **Applicability:** equational pairs; bounded saturation is incomplete.
- **Source:** Willsey et al., "egg: Fast and Extensible Equality Saturation", POPL 2021, DOI 10.1145/3434304; [BP] E-graphs ch. 25, https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/blueprint/egraphs-chapter.html; [Repo] `Generated/MagmaEgg/`; [Paper] §7.1.2.
- **Status here:** none — local BFS closures enumerate terms individually with no e-class sharing.
- **Executable:** Yes-bounded per saturation limit.

### C7. Proof reconstruction / trusted-certificate discipline
Never trust an ATP verdict; replay superposition steps via a Lean elaborator, e-graph explanations via refl/symm/trans/congruence, and validate everything through the kernel (lean4checker; no `native_decide`). Discovery engine and certificate checker are separated.
- **Side:** infra for both. **Applicability:** universal.
- **Source:** [Paper] §3, §4.7, §7.2 (Figs. 10–12; egg? + calcify); [Repo] `Superposition.lean`.
- **Status here:** structurally everywhere (the judge is the checker); explicitly in generalized's derivation-replay admission (`replay_derivation` + `admit`), m00006's local verification of LLM chains/tables before submission, reja's local `try_true`/`try_false` verification and sanitizers.
- **Executable:** Yes.

### C8. Derived-translation-map reasoning (L_x, R_x, squaring S)
Rewrite the law in terms of left/right translations and the squaring map; derive cancellation, injectivity/surjectivity relations, idempotent/absorption lemmas (e.g. `L_a R_b = id`), then chain them. The general-magma fragment of the ETP's hand and ATP proofs of hard implications.
- **Side:** TRUE. **Applicability:** laws exposing invertible/absorptive translations.
- **Source:** [Paper] §2, §7.3.8, §8; [BP] ch. 4 (hand derivations, e.g. Thm 4.5's ten-step 1689→2 chain).
- **Status here:** m00006 derived-law routes (`sandwich_left_projection`, `square_twist_comm`, absorption family); reja `standard_aux_superposition_attempt` (const/proj_l/proj_r/rowconst lemma ladder), `right_square_chain_body`, `square_sandwich_*`, `rowconst_body`; emily-family `_build_independence_lemma`.
- **Executable:** Yes-bounded per lemma library; open-ended derivation is Heuristic.

### C9. Projection-law reduction
When E1 is (or implies) a projection law `x◇y = x` or `x◇y = y`, both sides of E2 reduce recursively to their boundary variable; compare.
- **Side:** TRUE (and FALSE cue: projection witnesses). **Applicability:** projection-class E1.
- **Source:** special case of C1/C8; catalogued in ETP's equivalence classes ([Paper] §6.1).
- **Status here:** m00006 (`projection_law_route`, `projection_true_route`); dufius/eulerv5 analogues.
- **Executable:** Yes.

### C10. Hand analysis of hard implications
Bespoke human derivations for the residue: ETP ch. 4 named chains, §27.1's ten slow-ATP forward implications (450 ⇒ 413/426/432; 1119 ⇒ eight others).
- **Side:** TRUE. **Applicability:** residual pairs. **Source:** [BP] ch. 4, ch. 27.
- **Status here:** n/a (this is what the Frontier Forge ASSAULT step reproduces).
- **Executable:** Heuristic.

### C11. LLM-guided proof synthesis
Prompt an LLM for rewrite chains, guided chains, midpoint lemmas, or raw Lean; verify locally, then judge. ETP experience: marginal, except ChatGPT guessing E3523's complete rewriting system (verified afterwards).
- **Side:** both (proposal generator only). **Applicability:** anything, budget permitting.
- **Source:** [Paper] §11, §14; local competition protocol (`pipeline/proxy.py` PROMPT extraction).
- **Status here:** m00006 (4 response shapes; hop-level gap feedback `guided_chain_hop_unproved`, `frontier_bridge_hint`); reja (tool-driving protocol: strategy cards, midpoint/candidate-bundle/tool_call payloads, blackboard, judge-feedback loop); emily-family (calc-chain repair: `try_symm_repair`, `preflight_proof`, `parse_lean_error`); marathon fewshot (relevance-ranked few-shot prompting + pattern persistence); owen (pure LLM relay).
- **Executable:** Heuristic.

## D. Proving TRUE for finite magmas only (⊨fin; *not* the competition's TRUE)

### D1. Injectivity ⇔ surjectivity on finite sets

## E. Refuting with finite countermodels

### E1. Exhaustive enumeration of small magmas
All n^(n²) tables at n ≤ 4 (≈4.3×10⁹ magmas, 165 CPU-h): 13,632,566 refutations = 96.3% of all false order-≤4 implications, witnessed by **524 distinct magmas** (minimization procedure for that set: UNVERIFIED — the paper states only the count); size ≤ 3 covers 13,345,053. Size-5 exhaustion infeasible (~3×10¹⁷ even after iso+duality ≈ 240× savings). Kisielewicz: for the order-≤4 corpus, En ⊨ E2 iff En ⊨fin E2.
- **Side:** FALSE (and ⊭fin). **Applicability:** pairs with a witness of size ≤ 4.
- **Source:** [Paper] §5.1 (Table 1, Rem. 5.1); [Repo] `Generated/All4x4Tables/` (`src/brute_force_4x4_tables.c`, `data/plan.txt` per-magma check minimization, `generate_lean.py`).
- **Status here:** all solvers, capped small: m00006 `ENUMERATION_MAX_N = 3`; baseline/emily-family/eulerv5/dufius/suii0x n ≤ 3 (+ structured 4); marathon demos n ≤ 3.
- **Executable:** Yes-bounded.

### E2. Curated witness banks / selected-magma zoo
Hand-picked named magmas known to separate many laws: projections, constants, Boolean ops, cyclic groups, Kisielewicz ℕ-magmas (x+1, xy+1, prime-factorization tricks), string magmas, 𝔽₂ⁿ, quasigroups (all 1411 of size 5, via Mace4), central groupoids, cancellative 7–9 element hand-guided tables.
- **Side:** FALSE. **Applicability:** first-tier probe for any pair.
- **Source:** [BP] Selected magmas ch. 6, https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/blueprint/selected-magmas-chapter.html; [Paper] §5.
- **Status here:** m00006 named bank (LP/RP/C0/XOR/AND/OR/NAND/NOR/IMP/NIMP/XNOR/A2/Z3A/Z3B/T3L/T3R/S4A–F/S5A–D/**CG9**); eulerv5 `_table_bank` keyed by hypothesis ID; emily-family `known_counterexample` (pair-keyed); reja `witness_tables`.
- **Executable:** Yes-bounded.

### E3. Structured finite families
Parameterized incomplete families searched before generic backtracking: min/max semilattices, successor spines, conditional tables, negated-sum (−x−y, 1−x−y mod n), rectangular bands r×c, projection-with-exceptions, direct products of smaller witnesses. ETP found unstructured random size-5 sampling useless, structured (linear/quadratic/cancellative) sampling fruitful, commutative sampling disappointing.
- **Side:** FALSE. **Applicability:** any pair; family choice is the craft.
- **Source:** [Paper] §5.1–§5.2; [BP] ch. 6.
- **Status here:** m00006 (`structured families`, STRUCTURED_MAX_N = 7); suii0x (`rectangular_band_tables`, `projection_exception_tables`, `product_tables`); emily-family (`_structured_tables`, `_product_tables`, random 10,000); dufius (`_structured_families`, `_structured_size_4_families`); reja (`structured_tables`, `structured_counterexample_search` max_n 7 with dual).
- **Executable:** Yes-bounded per family.

### E4. Linear / affine magma models
`x◇y = ax + by (+c)` over ℤ/n, 𝔽_p, or noncommutative rings: every word evaluates to a linear form whose coefficients are polynomials in a,b; law E holds iff (a,b) lies in variety V_E; refute by (a,b) ∈ V_{E1} \ V_{E2}. Solvable by Gröbner bases (commutative) or the diamond lemma (noncommutative); Lefschetz + Chebotarev: any commutative-linear counterexample specializes to ℤ/pℤ (so also ⊭fin). Some pairs are provably immune to all linear models (E1485 ⊭ E151 → needs E9). ETP's brute force over linear ops refuted ~13.3M implications (mostly redundant with E1).
- **Side:** FALSE. **Applicability:** pairs separated on the linear variety.
- **Source:** [Paper] §5.2 (Exs. 5.2–5.3, Rems. 5.4–5.6); [Repo] `Generated/LinearOps/` (`src/brute_force_linear_equations.c`); pre-ETP named source for systematic linear counterexamples: NOT FOUND — UNVERIFIED as named prior technique (folklore in quasigroup theory).
- **Status here:** parameter *scans* only (no Gröbner/variety reasoning): m00006 affine families `(ax+by+c) mod n`; dufius `affine_table`/`try_affine_library`/`try_affine_separator`; reja poly tiers; eulerv5 structured tables.
- **Executable:** Yes-bounded (scan); Yes (Gröbner, for fixed degree).

### E5. Quadratic / polynomial magma models
`x◇y = ax² + bxy + cy² + dx + ey + f` over ℤ/N, small N; ETP found these "somewhat useful" beyond affine; degrades for large N (word polynomials have degree exponential in order).
- **Side:** FALSE. **Applicability:** pairs surviving linear scan.
- **Source:** [Paper] §5.2 Rem. 5.5; [Repo] `Generated/FinitePoly/` (`src/finite_poly_generate_lean.py`, randomly generated polynomial ops).
- **Status here:** m00006 quadratic families (+cxy, +cx², +cy² variants); reja `find_poly_counterexample` (general coefficient basis, n up to 18, tiered).
- **Executable:** Yes-bounded.

### E6. Finite model finders: SAT / SMT / MIP / Mace4-style ground search
Encode "table of size n satisfying E1, falsifying E2 at one assignment" and hand it to: Mace4 (ground equational rewriting; ETP's best at sizes 5–9, e.g. E677 size-9 model in 16 s; deep configuration lore §7.3.6 Tables 2–4), SAT solvers (CaDiCaL via eznf `scripts/generate_cadical/`; Glucose/MiniSAT/Kissat via PySAT), SMT (Z3 with uninterpreted sort, `scripts/generate_z3_counterexample.py`, emits self-validating Lean), MIP (HiGHS, `Generated/FinSearch/src/finsearch.py`), or Vampire's finite-model mode (`src/vampire_fin_counterexamples.py`). Paradox-lineage refinements: term definitions, incremental SAT, static symmetry reduction, sort inference (Claessen–Sörensson).
- **Side:** FALSE (⊭fin). **Applicability:** any pair, size by budget; decision procedure at fixed n.
- **Source:** McCune, "Mace4 Reference Manual and Guide", arXiv cs/0310055; [Paper] §5.6, §7.3.6; [Repo] paths above.
- **Status here:** reja only: `cp_sat_counterexample_search` (OR-tools CP-SAT, if importable), `sympy_sat_counterexample_search` (CNF via sympy). m00006 and all other demos have no constraint-solver encoding.
- **Executable:** Yes-bounded (fixed n); configuration Heuristic.

### E7. Constraint-propagating partial-table backtracking (SEM-style)
Assign cells in order; evaluate premise instances as soon as their cells are known; seed a goal-falsifying assignment first; propagate forced equalities; symmetry-break by canonical (least-number) value choices.
- **Side:** FALSE. **Applicability:** sizes 4–7 where enumeration dies.
- **Source:** MACE/SEM lineage (McCune above; Claessen–Sörensson Paradox, https://research.chalmers.se/en/publication/2547); local survey §"Constraint-propagating partial-table backtracking".
- **Status here:** reja `propagation_model_finder` (mf_propagate over partial tables) and `goal_directed_model_finder` (canonical Skolem assignments = symmetry breaking, goal seeded); emily-family `backtrack_counterexample` (sizes 4–5, time-limited); eulerv5 similar.
- **Executable:** Yes-bounded.

### E8. Isomorphism rejection / canonical search
Quotient the table search by carrier permutations (iso + duality ≈ 240× at n=5); Isofilter with custom discriminators turned a 10-day dedup into 0.02 s.
- **Side:** FALSE infra. **Source:** [Paper] §5.1, §7.3.9.
- **Status here:** partial — reja's `canonical_skolem_assignments`/`noncollapsed_skolem_order`; no local general iso-canonicalization.
- **Executable:** Yes.

### E9. Twisting semigroup invariant
Compute Twist_E, the semigroup generated by T,U under the relations that keep `x◇'y = Tx◇Uy` a model of E for endomorphisms T,U. If Twist_{E1} is not a quotient of Twist_{E2}, twist a Cartesian power M^{Twist_{E1}} by shift endomorphisms — the twisted magma satisfies E1 and usually violates E2 (must be checked). Signature win: E1485 ⊭ E151 via 𝔽₂⁵ with (x_i)◇'(y_i) = (1 − x_{i+1}·y_{i−1}) — "not easily refuted by any other method"; finite base ⇒ finite witness.
- **Side:** FALSE (⊭fin when base finite). **Applicability:** pairs with mismatched twisting semigroups; linear-immune pairs.
- **Source:** [Paper] §5.4 (Ex. 5.11); [BP] Weak central groupoids ch. 12 §12.1.
- **Status here:** none.
- **Executable:** Twist computation Yes; refutation check Yes; overall Semi (final violation not guaranteed).

### E10. Magma cohomology / affine extensions
Extend base G (satisfying both laws) by abelian M via `(x,s)◇(y,t) = (x◇_G y, as+bt+f(x,y))`; E1 holds iff f satisfies a *linear* cocycle equation; refute by f ∈ Z²_{E1} \ Z²_{E2} — pure linear algebra over the base. Example: E1110 ⊭ E1629 via a 25-element extension over 𝔽₅ (base x◇y = 3x−y, 6-dimensional cocycle space). Combines with table surgery (E1659 ⊭ E4315). "Higher magma cohomology" flagged open.
- **Side:** FALSE (⊭fin for finite bases). **Applicability:** E1 with a manageable base model (e.g. linear) admitting nontrivial cocycles.
- **Source:** [Paper] §5.6(ii) (Props. 5.15–5.16); [BP] Magma cohomology ch. 11, https://teorth.github.io/equational_theories/blueprint/cohomology-chapter.html.
- **Status here:** none. Closest relative: reja `skew_product_counterexample_search` (control×fiber tables with an affine fiber library and violation-batch repair) builds extension-*shaped* tables but does not solve cocycle equations.
- **Executable:** Yes-bounded (linear algebra per base/module choice); ansatz choice Heuristic.

### E11. Base-model modification: table surgery
Start from M ⊨ E1∧E2, flip a few cells to break E2, repair E1 (by hand, ATP, or SAT). Especially productive on "mutable" laws (E854).
- **Side:** FALSE. **Applicability:** perturbation-tolerant laws.
- **Source:** [Paper] §5.6(i); [BP] §7.5 Thm 7.17 (1659 ⊭ 4315).
- **Status here:** partial — reja `local_search_route` (seeded local search over tables) and the skew-product violation-repair loop are in this spirit; no principled "start from a model of both" surgery anywhere.
- **Executable:** Heuristic with mechanical subroutines.

### E12. Base-model modification: enlargement & "squares-first"
Embed M as a submagma of a larger M′, choosing new blocks to preserve E1 and kill E2. Refinement used for E1729 ⊭ E817 (the hardest ETP refutation, months of work, >4000 Lean lines): choose the square-set S_M first, then the squaring map, then the S_M×N / N×S_M blocks, then N×N, greedily at each stage.
- **Side:** FALSE. **Applicability:** laws structured around the squaring operator.
- **Source:** [Paper] §5.6(iii), §1.3; [BP] E1729 ch. 18 (Thm 18.1 extension theorem, Thm 18.19); [Repo] `ManuallyProved/Equation1729/`.
- **Status here:** none.
- **Executable:** Heuristic.

### E13. Dynamical / evolutionary magma search
Evolve a population of finite magmas: knock out random cells and let a SAT solver/ATP re-complete under E1 (possibly growing the carrier); inject an explicit E2-violation into emptied cells and seek consistent completion; "adiabatic forcing" — impose selected *consequences* of E1 instead of E1 to drift toward satisfaction. Wins: E854 ⊭fin E413/E1045/E1055/E3316/E3925. Fails when finite models of E1 are rare (E677).
- **Side:** FALSE (⊭fin). **Applicability:** laws with abundant finite models.
- **Source:** [Paper] §5.6 (tools: Glucose, Kissat, Mace4, Vampire-as-solver).
- **Status here:** none (reja's violation-batch skew repair is a distant cousin).
- **Executable:** Heuristic pipeline, mechanical steps.

### E14. Translation-invariant finite models over group tables
`x◇y = x + f(y−x)` on finite abelian groups, or `x◇y = x·f(x⁻¹y)` on nonabelian ones; scan f over a group from the GAP small-groups library (SAGE + Mace4 assist). Reduces the law to a functional equation in ≤ n−1 variables — trivial for 1-variable laws, productive for 2.
- **Side:** FALSE (⊭fin). **Applicability:** ≤ 2 essential variables after normalization; note the squaring map is forced invertible, so some pairs are immune.
- **Source:** [Paper] §5.3, §7.3.4; [BP] Infinite constructions ch. 7 §7.1 (shared machinery).
- **Status here:** none as a group-based family. Adjacent: reja `residue_ray_countermodel_attempt` (residue-class-conditional ops over ℤ/m rendered as Lean artifacts) and m00006's negated-sum tables are special cases of translation-invariant ops on ℤ/n.
- **Executable:** Yes-bounded per group.

### E15. Central groupoid theory (A² = J) and targeted spectrum witnesses
For E168-type laws `x = (y◇x)◇(x◇z)` / `(x◇y)◇(y◇z) = y`: finite central groupoids ↔ 0-1 matrices with A² = J (unique length-2 walks digraph); every finite one has order n² with exactly n idempotents; "natural" ones live on S×S with (a,b)◇(c,d) = (b,c) and are characterized by the extra axiom (a◇(a◇a))◇b = a◇b; non-natural ones exist from order 4 up (Knuth's machine search; labeled counts 1, 12, 1,330,560 for k = 1,2,3). Derived rewrite consequences (a◇((a◇b)◇c) → a◇b) prove TRUE-side facts; the n² spectrum plus non-natural witnesses refute "pseudo-consequences" that hold in all natural central groupoids. ETP's Lean form: `MagmaA2T : Magma (Fin 9)` (Knuth's A2 flattened) with `MagmaA2T.Facts` satisfying 13 equations and refuting 32.
- **Side:** both (rewrite consequences TRUE; witnesses + spectrum FALSE).
- **Applicability:** central-groupoid-class hypotheses (E168; relatives incl. weak central groupoids E1485).
- **Source:** D. E. Knuth, "Notes on Central Groupoids", J. Combinatorial Theory 8 (1970) 376–390; [Repo] `equational_theories/CentralGroupoids.lean` (MagmaA21/MagmaA2/MagmaA2T, `equiv_MagmaA2_MagmaA2T`, Facts at lines 121–125); [BP] ch. 4 Thm 4.9. Post-Knuth line (all FALSE-side classification/counting): Hoffman's 1967 A²=J problem (open); Lam–van Lint, "Directed graphs with unique paths of fixed length", JCTB 1978 (author list UNVERIFIED page-level); Curtis–Drew–Li–Pragel, JCTA 105 (2004) 35–50; Kündgen–Leander–Thomassen, JCTA 118 (2011) 2025–2034 (switching conjecture disproved; extension theorem); orderly enumeration arXiv math/0407070, OEIS A283627; Etzion, J. Graph Theory 2024; arXiv 2607.24962 (2026).
- **Status here:** m00006 ships **CG9**, a non-natural order-9 central groupoid witness, placed last in the named bank (the eq168-wall episode); no local solver constructs A²=J matrices on demand or uses the n² spectrum generally.
- **Executable:** matrix search Yes-bounded; n² theorem closed-form; general classification open.

### E16. Countermodel minimization and canonicalization
Keep the falsifying assignment; restrict to the submagma it generates; relabel canonically; recheck; emit the smallest table. ETP's `plan.txt` analogue minimizes which laws each magma must be checked against in Lean.
- **Side:** FALSE infra. **Source:** [Repo] `Generated/All4x4Tables/data/plan.txt` + `make-plan`; local survey §"Countermodel minimization".
- **Status here:** none (solvers emit the first verified table).
- **Executable:** Yes.

## F. Refuting by syntactic invariants (no explicit search)

### F1. Variable-multiplicity balance (SameCount)
If every variable occurs equally often on both sides of E1, every consequence has the same property (the multiset/free-abelian magma realizes the obstruction); variants: multiplicities mod m via free abelian exponent-m groups; order mismatch.
- **Side:** FALSE. **Applicability:** syntactic mismatch between E1 (balanced) and E2 (unbalanced).
- **Source:** [BP] ch. 3 Thm 3.8 (`Law.MagmaLaw.SameCount.derive`); [Paper] §6.2 Ex. 6.4, §1.3.
- **Status here:** as *triage only*: reja `symbolic_invariant_report` (occurrence signatures, mod-m), dufius `try_separators` (invariant-driven witness selection). No local solver converts an invariant hit directly into a certificate (locals must realize it as a finite table anyway; cyclic linear magmas usually do).
- **Executable:** Yes.

### F2. Matching invariants / metatheorems from lifting magma families
General recipe: any computable map I on free-magma terms with I constant on E1-consequence classes refutes E1 ⊨ E2 when I separates E2's sides. Instances: leftmost/rightmost variable (via E4/E5 free magmas), ordered leaf tuple (associativity), variable sets, multisets, images in any tractable relative free magma; formalized as "lifting magma families" generating bulk non-implications automatically.
- **Side:** FALSE. **Applicability:** invariant-separable pairs (largely subsumed by small models, but cheap).
- **Source:** [BP] Metatheorems ch. 9 (Meta-metatheorem 9.1, Metathms 9.2–9.6, Def 9.7 lifting families, Thm 9.9); [Paper] §6.2 (Prop. 6.1, Exs. 6.2–6.4); [Repo] `Generated/InvariantMetatheoremNonimplications/`, `LiftingMagmaFamilies.lean`.
- **Status here:** boundary-variable features exist as *routing* signals (m00006 `boundary_vars`; dufius leftmost/rightmost); not as refutation certificates.
- **Executable:** Yes per invariant.

### F3. Canonizers
A computable C: FreeMagma → FreeMagma constant on ∼E1 classes, built from a weakly-collapsing, non-overlapping reduction (Thm 6.9's syntactic conditions); refutes whenever C separates E2's sides. The normal-form map of any terminating confluent system is a canonizer.
- **Side:** FALSE (with completion: full decision, cf. C4).
- **Applicability:** E1 whose LHS→RHS reduction is non-overlapping.
- **Source:** [Paper] §6.3 (Defs. 6.6–6.7, Thm 6.9); [BP] ch. 10 §10.1 (Cor 10.7 normal-form criterion, Thm 10.8 E477); [Repo] `Generated/Confluence/` (`src/find_confluent_equations.py` — laws x = E where E cannot match its own subterms), `Confluence*.lean`.
- **Status here:** none.
- **Executable:** Yes once R found; devising R Heuristic (often mechanical via the non-self-matching check).

### F4. Free-magma structure theory / unique factorization (E854 pattern)
For left-absorptive laws `x ≃ x◇f(x,y,z)`: characterize ∼E on irreducible words, prove unique factorization in the relative free magma, then decide specific non-equivalences directly (E854 ⊭ E3316 — later superseded by finite counterexamples).
- **Side:** FALSE. **Applicability:** the specific absorptive family; template for others.
- **Source:** [Paper] §6.4 (Thm 6.11, Cor 6.12, Prop 6.13); [BP] E854 ch. 14 (Thm 14.1, Thm 14.14).
- **Status here:** none.
- **Executable:** Heuristic (human theory-building, mechanical checks).

## G. Refuting with infinite models (outside the competition's finite-witness FALSE contract)

### G1. Translation-invariant infinite magmas
`x◇y = x + f(y−x)` on ℤ or another infinite abelian group (nonabelian: x·f(x⁻¹y)); the law becomes a functional equation for f. E.g. f = −sgn on ℤ proves E1648 ⊭ E206.
- **Side:** FALSE (infinite). **Applicability:** ≤ 2 essential variables; squaring forced invertible.
- **Source:** [Paper] §5.3 (Ex. 5.7); [BP] ch. 7 §7.1–7.3 (Asterix/Obelix reductions).
- **Status here:** none (reja could *submit* such a model via G6 if an LLM produced it; no generator).
- **Executable:** Heuristic (functional-equation analysis).

### G2. Greedy constructions
Build a countable magma as the direct limit of finite partial tables: seed an E2-violation; maintain a ruleset Γ sound for E1 in the limit; prove a greedy extension property (any undefined product can be filled, adding fresh elements). Iterative rule discovery with ATP vetting (if ATP proves Γ+rule ⊢ E2, reject the rule). The ETP's most productive infinite-countermodel method (E73 ⊭ E4380 worked example; E677 ⊭ E255; E1722, E713, E1289, E63, E1076, E3308; Asterix/Obelix; Dupont/Dupond). Mechanized in-repo: `forcer.py` reads per-equation forcing rules, verifies with Vampire (2 s timeouts), and `forcer_leanify.py` emits Lean.
- **Side:** FALSE (inherently infinite — never refutes ⊨fin).
- **Applicability:** hard residual pairs; rules per-equation.
- **Source:** [Paper] §5.5 (Thm 5.12, Prop. 5.13), §7.2.2, §7.3.7; [BP] ch. 7 §7.4 (Lemmas 7.10–7.15), chs. 13–17 case studies; [Repo] `Generated/Greedy/` + `src/forcer.py`, `data/forcing_rules/*.rules`, `AdjoinFresh.lean`, `Mathlib/Order/Greedy.lean`.
- **Status here:** none.
- **Executable:** Heuristic core (ruleset design), mechanical ATP subroutines.

### G3. Translation-invariant greedy
Greedily build the partial function f on a growing (freely extended) group — abelian or nonabelian — satisfying the functional-equation ruleset (second proof of E73 ⊭ E4380; Dupont via T³−T = −I on ℤ³×ℤ).
- **Side:** FALSE (infinite). **Source:** [Paper] §5.5 Thm 5.14; [BP] §7.6.
- **Status here:** none. **Executable:** Heuristic.

### G4. Ad hoc infinite constructions
Reduced words on free products (3-regular tree on ℤ₂*ℤ₂*ℤ₂ for E206 ⊭ E1648 — inherently infinite since E206 ≡fin E1648); polynomial rings (ℤ[t], P ↦ tP for E3342); shift operators on ℤ^ℕ (noncommutative-linear, E1117 ⊭ E2441); Kisielewicz prime-factorization magmas on ℕ; magma-induced digraphs/partial orders guiding constructions. Roughly two dozen sui generis constructions, detailed only in the blueprint.
- **Side:** FALSE (infinite). **Source:** [Paper] §5.3 Ex. 5.8, Rem. 5.9, §1.3; [BP] §7.7, ch. 13 (E677 free-magma-on-trees), ch. 27.
- **Status here:** none. **Executable:** Heuristic.

### G5. E1729 extension theorem
Base = countable abelian group of exponent 4 extended by a free nonabelian group of "non-squares"; the extension theorem (Thm 18.1) + squares-first greedy settles E1729 ⊭ E817.
- **Side:** FALSE (infinite). **Source:** [BP] ch. 18; [Repo] `ManuallyProved/Equation1729/`.
- **Status here:** none. **Executable:** Heuristic.

### G6. LLM-supplied symbolic / type-level model artifacts
Accept from an LLM a structured Lean model plan (carrier, definitions, operation, hypothesis proof, counterexample proof — patchable in parts) or a whole Lean artifact for a symbolic, possibly infinite countermodel, sanitize and judge it. This is a local innovation of the *protocol* (the mathematics it can carry is G1–G5 prior art).
- **Side:** FALSE. **Applicability:** limited by the judge's Goal type (no finiteness hypothesis — see local survey §"Precise framing").
- **Source:** [Local] reja `EQT02-S00023.py`: `is_infinite_model_payload`, `SYMBOLIC_MODEL_PLAN_KINDS`/`PATCH_KINDS`, `judge_infinite_model_artifact_attributed`, symbolic family tables (`table_from_symbolic_family`, `safe_model_family_expr`).
